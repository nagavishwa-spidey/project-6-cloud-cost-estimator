Cloud Economics & FinOps Learning Guide

Understanding billing mechanics, egress economics, and cost optimization strategies.

1. How Compute Billing Works

Virtual Machines (like AWS EC2, Azure VMs, GCP Compute Engine) bill on a per-second or per-hour basis while in an active running state. When an instance is stopped, compute charges pause, but attached persistent disks continue to incur storage costs.

  • On-Demand: Maximum flexibility, pay-per-second, highest unit cost.
  • Reserved / Savings Plans: 1-3 year commitments with up to 72% discounts.
  • Spot Instances: Spare cloud capacity with up to 90% savings, subject to eviction.

2. The Storage Hierarchy

Storage costs depend heavily on access speed and durability:

  • Standard Object Storage (S3 / Blob): High durability, active access, ~₹1.90 / GB / month.
  • Block Storage (EBS / Managed Disks): Attached virtual hard drives, billed for provisioned capacity regardless of utilization.
  • Cold / Archive Storage: Long-term data retention at a fraction of the cost, but subject to retrieval fees and delays.

3. The Hidden Cost: Network Egress

Data transferred INTO cloud data centers is almost universally free. However, data transferred OUT to the public internet or across different cloud regions or availability zones carries significant per-gigabyte egress charges.

  • Major providers offer ~100 GB/month free internet egress.
  • Deploying a CDN (e.g. CloudFront, Cloudflare) dramatically slashes egress costs by caching assets closer to users.

4. Practical FinOps Cost Optimization

Four actionable practices to prevent unexpected cloud bills:

  • Right-Sizing: Monitor CPU and memory; downgrade oversized instances running at under 20% utilization.
  • Auto-Stopping: Automatically shut down non-production development and staging environments outside office hours.
  • Storage Lifecycle Policies: Transition older files automatically from Standard to Archival tiers after 30-90 days.
  • Billing Budgets & Alerts: Set proactive cost threshold alerts to prevent billing surprises.
